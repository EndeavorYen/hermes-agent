# Bright Clean PICO-8 Cover Notes

Use this reference when the user requests an 8-bit/PICO-8/game-like storybook or podcast cover and rejects dark/noisy pixel art.

## Preferred look

- 16:9 landscape cover/thumbnail.
- Bright daytime palette: sky blue, saturated grass, warm cream/yellow accents, clear character colors.
- Fantasy-console / PICO-8-inspired feel: crisp chunky pixel shapes, simplified shading, clean silhouettes.
- Main story characters unobstructed, usually right side or center-right.
- Editable local typography, not AI-baked text.
- Light cream/mint information panel for podcast metadata instead of a dark overlay.

## Avoid

- Dark overlay, smoky haze, vignette, night filter, heavy shadow mask.
- Heavy dithering/noise/grain/scanlines that reads as dirty or low quality.
- Busy foreground objects blocking faces or title space.
- AI-generated text, pseudo-letters, watermark, logo artifacts.
- Excessively strict low-resolution pixel constraints if they hurt readability; polished PICO-8-inspired is acceptable.

## Reusable podcast cover geometry

Keep the current story-podcast template fixed unless explicitly redesigned:

1. Right-side story illustration.
2. Left rounded information panel.
3. Top series pill: `酥雞故事 Podcast`.
4. Play icon near panel edge.
5. Large Traditional Chinese title.
6. White/light rounded subtitle pill.
7. Accent divider line.
8. Episode label and small metadata line.

For bright game-style episodes, the panel should be light/translucent and should not darken the whole image.

## QC checklist

- [ ] Looks bright and vivid at thumbnail size.
- [ ] Characters and key story object are unobstructed.
- [ ] Traditional Chinese text is readable on mobile.
- [ ] No dark mask/overlay; no dirty dithering/noise.
- [ ] No watermark or random generated text.
- [ ] Child-friendly mood; wolves/antagonists are not scary unless the story requires it.
